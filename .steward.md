# STEWARD repository law

STEWARD is the product/composition/integration root for Aftergraph's persistent governed intelligence system.

Non-negotiables:
- do not duplicate AIE authority ownership;
- do not duplicate Trust Gateway enforcement/credential ownership;
- do not duplicate Runtime scheduling truth;
- do not duplicate WORKS durable execution truth;
- do not duplicate Sentinel verification truth;
- selected != authorized;
- completed != verified;
- execution context != authority;
- verification binds to an exact immutable subject;
- every consequential effect must be live-authority-revalidated and governed at the effect boundary.
